Questions, answered

Before you begin

Is Le Destin a dating app?

Le Destin is a curated matchmaking and experiences platform. Technology supports the journey, while the emphasis stays on considered profiles, real introductions and genuine face-to-face connection.

What is Match Credit?

Match Credit is used to access participating Le Destin games, introductions and experiences. It may be purchased, gifted or included with eligible memberships and campaigns.

Does every applicant receive a profile?

Applications are reviewed to maintain a thoughtful community. Successful candidates are notified to submit photographs and complete the next steps.

What happens at a Fish Pool event?

It is a curated group experience designed to make introductions natural through guided conversations, games and shared moments—without awkward speed-dating pressure.

Where are experiences held?

Experiences take place at selected venues across Singapore. Each invitation includes its exact location, format and Match Credit requirement.
